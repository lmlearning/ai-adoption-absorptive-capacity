# Data provenance

- `practical_data_survey_2026.parquet`: 1,101 responses from the [Practical Data Community survey](https://joereis.github.io/practical_data_data_eng_survey/).
- `stackoverflow_2025_survey.csv`: downloaded on demand from the [official Stack Exchange survey archive](https://github.com/StackExchange/Survey/tree/32a114542da67e3759479637343718502742adfd/packages/archive/2025). The downloader pins commit `32a114542da67e3759479637343718502742adfd` and verifies SHA-256 `2d1f65308877282edfb4470520eabbc08cb499118432a3dcec6a66c086aa2baa` (140,893,245 bytes), taken from the publisher's Git LFS pointer.
- `stackoverflow_2025_schema.csv`: accompanying question codebook supplied with this research repository.
- The `*_analysis_results.json` files are historical exploratory summaries. The paper's exported tables live in [outputs](../outputs/) and are generated by [run_all.py](../code/run_all.py).

The Stack Overflow microdata has 49,191 rows; the publisher's [methodology page](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/methodology/) reports 49,009 qualified responses. The paper explains its use of the public microdata and question-specific denominators. These populations must not be interchanged.
